# 🌟 Kids Daily Routine

A daily routine and checklist planner for kids, running from wake-up to bedtime, with separate school-day and holiday schedules that switch automatically.

**[Open the live planner →](#)** *(replace this link with your github.io URL)*

---

## What it does

- **Wake-to-sleep daily routine** — a full checklist including water breaks, reading, park time, and game/screen time.
- **Automatic school vs holiday routines** — set your term dates once, and the right routine (school day or holiday) loads automatically based on the date.
- **Multiple children** — add, remove, or rename children; each has their own checklist and progress, tracked separately per day.
- **Reading log** — record pages read per child, per day, with a running history.
- **Term Dates** — mark school terms and holidays so the right routine loads automatically.
- **Extracurricular activities** — add recurring weekly activities (day, time, location) that appear automatically on the right days.
- **Star rewards & progress bar** — kids can tick off activities themselves and watch their progress fill up.
- **Fully customisable** — every routine, category, and child is editable, so any family can make this their own.
- **Works offline, installable** — add it to a phone or tablet home screen like a real app.

## Getting started

1. Open the live link above.
2. On a phone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone/Safari) or the ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen (Android/Chrome).
3. On desktop: use your browser's Install app option, or just bookmark it.
4. Rename the default children to your own kids' names, and set your term dates so the right routine loads automatically.

Data is saved locally in each device's own browser, so it stays private.
